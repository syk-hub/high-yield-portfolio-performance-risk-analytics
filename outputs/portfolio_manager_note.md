**Important limitations:** Prices, holdings, duration, and OAS are
simulated. Allocations were chosen after reviewing simulated returns.

eligibility: bond-level evidence remains incomplete, including unresolved
identifiers, ratings, call details, and outstanding-principal checks.
The constructed benchmark is not a market index. SQL checks test coverage
and arithmetic consistency against supplied/imported figures; they do not
verify source evidence, market prices, eligibility, or the validity of
simulation assumptions. This note is educational, not investment advice.
